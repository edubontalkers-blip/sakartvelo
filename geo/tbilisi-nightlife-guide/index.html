Tbilisi Nightlife Guide 2026: Best Clubs and Bars for Every Traveler

🇬🇪 Georgia Travel Guide  ·  Published: July 09, 2026  ·  sakartvelo.ai

Tbilisi has quietly become one of Europe's most exciting nightlife destinations, drawing travelers from around the world with its raw energy, underground culture, and famously welcoming locals. Whether you're chasing hard-hitting techno or a relaxed evening with natural wine, the Georgian capital delivers an experience unlike anywhere else.

The undisputed centerpiece of Tbilisi nightlife is Bassiani, a techno club built inside the swimming pool halls beneath Dinamo Arena stadium. It regularly hosts world-class DJs and is considered one of the top clubs in Europe by Resident Advisor. Doors typically open late — expect the real action to begin well after midnight. Entry prices range from around 20 to 40 GEL (approximately 7–14 USD) depending on the lineup. Bassiani enforces a strict no-photo policy on the dance floor, so leave your phone in your pocket and lose yourself in the music. The club is located near Varketili metro station, making it reasonably accessible by metro or taxi.

Just as legendary is Mtkvarze, a riverside club perched above the Mtkvari River, offering a more eclectic mix of electronic music, live sets, and occasional cultural events. The outdoor terrace makes it especially popular in warmer months. For a slightly more intimate underground experience, Cafe Gallery in the Fabrika complex hosts regular nights that blend art, music, and nightlife in a post-Soviet textile factory setting — a very Tbilisi combination.

Fabrika itself, located in the Chugureti neighborhood, is worth visiting even before midnight. The converted Soviet factory courtyard is packed with bars, food stalls, and creative spaces that buzz from early evening onward. It's a perfect starting point for any night out.

If you prefer bars over clubs, the bars Old Town has to offer are a real highlight. Stroll through the winding streets of Abanotubani and Kala and you'll stumble upon dozens of intimate wine bars, craft beer spots, and cocktail lounges. Vino Underground on Galaktion Tabidze Street is a beloved stop for natural Georgian wine served by knowledgeable staff in a relaxed cellar setting. Wine prices here start around 8–12 GEL per glass. Nearby, 8000 Vintages is another excellent spot for exploring the deep tradition of Georgian qvevri wines.

For craft beer lovers, Lager Bar near Rustaveli Avenue has built a loyal following with a rotating selection of local Georgian craft brews and a no-frills atmosphere that locals love.

A few practical tips before you head out. Dress codes in Tbilisi clubs are enforced more by vibe than strict rules — avoid overtly sporty or sloppy clothing at upscale venues. Always carry cash, as some smaller bars and clubs do not accept cards. Taxis are cheap and widely available through the Bolt app, which is the most reliable option for getting home safely after a long night. A cross-city ride rarely costs more than 10–15 GEL.

Finally, be aware that Tbilisi nightlife runs very late by international standards. Showing up to a club before 1 AM will often mean an empty dance floor. Embrace the pace, start with dinner and wine, explore the bars Old Town offers as a warm-up, and let the night unfold naturally. That relaxed attitude is exactly what makes clubs Tbilisi has built its reputation on so special.
