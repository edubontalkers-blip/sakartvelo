Best Hotels in Batumi 2026 — Where to Stay in Georgia's Black Sea Gem

🇬🇪 Georgia Travel Guide  ·  Published: July 12, 2026  ·  sakartvelo.ai

Batumi, the vibrant capital of the Adjara region on Georgia's Black Sea coast, has transformed into one of the Caucasus's most exciting travel destinations. With its striking mix of Belle Époque architecture, modern towers, and a lively seaside boulevard, choosing where to stay in Batumi can genuinely shape your whole trip. Whether you are chasing luxury, comfort, or value, the city has accommodation to match every style and budget.

**The Batumi Boulevard Area — Best for First-Time Visitors**

If it is your first time in Batumi, staying near the famous Batumi Boulevard is the smartest move. This 7-kilometer seafront promenade is the beating heart of the city, lined with cafes, fountains, and the iconic Alphabet Tower. Hotels here put you within easy walking distance of the Old Town, the central beach, and the casino strip.

The Sheraton Batumi Hotel is one of the city's most recognized properties, offering sea-view rooms, a rooftop pool, and reliable international-standard service. Expect rates starting around 180–250 USD per night during high season (June–August). For a more boutique feel, Hotel Paragraphi near the Old Town is a well-reviewed mid-range option with rates closer to 60–90 USD per night, offering charming rooms in a restored historic building.

**Old Town Batumi — Atmosphere and Character**

Batumi's Old Town is compact but full of personality, with wrought-iron balconies, hidden courtyards, and excellent Georgian restaurants. Staying here means you are steps from Piazza Square and the local wine bars. Guesthouses in this neighborhood, such as the well-regarded Old Town Boutique Hotel, typically charge 50–80 USD per night and offer an authentic, locally rooted experience that larger hotels simply cannot replicate.

**Luxury Options in Adjara**

For travelers seeking premium accommodation in Adjara, Batumi has expanded its luxury offerings significantly. The Radisson Blu Hotel Batumi remains a flagship property with panoramic Black Sea views, a spa, multiple restaurants, and spacious rooms. High-season rates generally start at around 200 USD per night. The Wyndham Grand Batumi is another strong contender in the luxury segment, known for its central location and polished amenities.

**Budget-Friendly Stays**

Batumi is genuinely affordable compared to most European coastal cities. Dozens of family-run guesthouses and small hotels operate throughout the city, particularly in residential streets just a few blocks from the seafront. Expect to find clean, comfortable rooms with breakfast included for as little as 25–45 USD per night. Booking platforms like Booking.com and local Georgian travel sites often feature these smaller Batumi hotels, which are ideal for longer stays.

**Practical Tips for Booking Accommodation**

High season in Batumi runs from late June through August, when the city fills with visitors from across Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, and Turkey. Book your accommodation at least two to three months in advance if you are traveling during this window. Shoulder season — May, September, and early October — offers better prices and far smaller crowds while the weather remains pleasant.

When comparing where to stay in Batumi, consider proximity to the beach if sea access matters to you, and check whether your hotel includes parking, as traffic in the city center can be challenging. Most reputable Batumi hotels accept card payments, though having some Georgian Lari on hand is always advisable for smaller guesthouses and local markets.

Batumi rewards visitors who take time to explore beyond the neon lights, and the right hotel is your base for doing exactly that.
