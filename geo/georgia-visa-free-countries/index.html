Georgia Visa Free: Which Countries Can Enter and What You Need to Know

🇬🇪 Georgia Travel Guide  ·  Published: July 07, 2026  ·  sakartvelo.ai

Georgia, the jewel of the South Caucasus, has become one of the most exciting travel destinations in recent years. With its ancient monasteries, dramatic mountain landscapes, world-class wine, and famously warm hospitality, it is easy to understand why visitor numbers keep climbing. The good news for most travelers is that getting in is remarkably straightforward.

Georgia maintains one of the most open visa policies in the region, allowing citizens of a large number of countries to travel to Georgia without a visa entirely. Understanding the rules before you board your flight will save you time, stress, and potentially money.

**Who Can Enter Georgia Visa Free?**

Citizens of all European Union member states, the United States, Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, Japan, South Korea, and many other countries can enter Georgia visa free for stays of up to 365 days. Yes, you read that correctly — a full year without needing to apply for any permit in advance. This extraordinarily generous policy has made Georgia a favorite among digital nomads and long-term travelers.

The complete and official list of eligible nationalities is published by the Government of Georgia and is updated periodically, so it is always worth verifying your specific country on the official Georgian e-Visa portal (www.evisa.gov.ge) or through your nearest Georgian embassy before traveling.

**Visa on Arrival for Other Nationalities**

Citizens of countries not on the visa-free list may still be eligible for a visa on arrival at Tbilisi International Airport and certain land border crossings. The visa on arrival typically costs around 20 USD and allows a stay of up to 90 days. You will need a valid passport, proof of sufficient funds, and ideally a return ticket or onward travel documentation. Processing is generally quick, but having the fee ready in cash is advisable.

Some nationalities are required to obtain a visa in advance through the Georgian e-Visa system. The e-Visa fee is currently 20 USD, applications are processed within five business days, and approval is sent directly to your email.

**Practical Tips for Arriving in Georgia**

Regardless of your entry method, your passport must be valid for the entire duration of your intended stay. Georgia does not enforce a strict minimum validity period beyond that, but carrying a passport with at least six months remaining is always good practice.

If you plan to travel to Georgia without a visa under the 365-day allowance, be aware that overstaying is taken seriously. Fines apply for exceeding your permitted period, and repeat violations can result in entry bans.

The main international entry points are Tbilisi International Airport, Kutaisi International Airport, and several land border crossings with Armenia, Azerbaijan, and Turkey. The Lars crossing into Russia is currently not recommended due to the ongoing political situation.

**Why the Open Policy Matters for Your Trip**

Georgia's welcoming visa policy reflects the country's broader attitude toward guests. The Georgian phrase "Guests are a gift from God" is not just a saying — it is a lived reality. Whether you arrive for a weekend city break in Tbilisi or an extended mountain trek in Svaneti, the bureaucratic barriers are refreshingly low.

Always double-check the latest entry requirements closer to your travel date, as policies can change. With the right preparation, your journey into this remarkable country will begin smoothly the moment you land.
